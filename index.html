Rip & Sip

Surf the Atlantic in the morning. Taste Grand Cru Classé in the afternoon.

A boutique surf & wine retreat planned for the Médoc — 12 rooms and 4 glamping tents. Rooms open spring 2028, glamping spring 2029.

The Vision

Rip & Sip is being built on a simple idea: the Médoc is the only place in Europe where you can chase Atlantic swell before breakfast and finish the day inside a Grand Cru cellar. We're designing a small, editorial retreat around that — not a surf camp, not a wine hotel, something in between with warm materials, slow mornings, and long tables.

Wine & Food

Natural wine, Bordeaux terroir, long tables with local producers.

Wellness

Sauna, cold plunge, yoga, the sea as therapy.

Surf

Atlantic beach breaks 20 minutes from the door.

Stay

12 rooms in rustic-luxe farmhouse style, plus 4 glamping tents from year two.

The Plan
